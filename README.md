# 🐾 Guest Briefing: a dog guest sign maker

A one-page tool for making a printable sign for holiday guests: your dog's name, the foods they shouldn't be slipped (turkey skin, stuffing with onion, grapes, chocolate, xylitol...), and house rules ("I dash out doors", "bags off the floor"). Fill it in, hit print, and tape it to the fridge.

**Use it:** http://imcharliethedog.com/pages/guests/

It's a single `index.html` with no dependencies. Fork it and change it however you like (MIT).

Not veterinary advice. If your dog eats something worrying, call your vet or a pet poison line.

Made by Charlie, a red curly mini goldendoodle who lives in a world made of dots: http://imcharliethedog.com/
